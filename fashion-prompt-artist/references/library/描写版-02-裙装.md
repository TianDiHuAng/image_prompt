# 描写版 02 · 裙装 · 连衣裙（具体描写）

> **裙装描写公式**：廓形与长度 → 腰线处理 → 裙摆动态（行走/风/旋转）→ 面料光感 → 细节（开衩/褶/荷叶边/拖尾）。

## 半身裙
- **A-line skirt A字裙** — 腰部贴合、自臀线向下散开成A字，裙摆轻盈微荡，行走时摆出柔和弧线 | *A-line skirt fitted at the waist, flaring gently from the hips, hem swaying softly with each step*
- **pleated mini skirt 百褶短裙** — 细百褶从腰头密排到裙摆，转体时褶片开合闪烁，裙摆展成扇形 | *knife-pleated mini skirt, pleats opening and flashing as she turns, hem fanning wide*
- **tennis skirt 网球裙** — 短至大腿中段，裙摆微翘带弹性，运动感利落 | *tennis skirt at mid-thigh, hem flicking up slightly, sporty and crisp*
- **pencil skirt 铅笔裙** — 贴合臀腿线条至膝收窄，行走步幅受限，开衩随步微张 | *pencil skirt hugging hips and thighs, tapering to the knee, slit easing open with each step*
- **satin slip skirt 缎面半裙** — 沿腿部斜裁垂落，布面光带随移动流动，液体感 | *bias-cut satin slip skirt, bands of light flowing with movement, liquid drape*
- **tulle skirt 纱裙** — 多层网纱蓬起，层叠处透出深浅，行走时纱层飘散分离 | *layered tulle skirt, translucency shifting between layers, gauze drifting apart mid-step*
- **tutu skirt 芭蕾纱裙** — 短而横展，硬纱撑出圆盘轮廓，腰头紧收 | *tutu skirt held wide in a stiff disc, tightly gathered waist*
- **mermaid skirt 鱼尾裙** — 贴身至膝，膝下突然散开成喇叭，行走时下摆甩出弧线 | *mermaid skirt clinging to the knee then bursting into a flare, hem swinging wide*
- **thigh-high slit maxi skirt 高开叉长裙** — 长裙垂坠拖曳，开衩随步伐张合，腿部线条隐现 | *maxi skirt with a thigh-high slit, opening and closing with each stride, leg glimpsed between*
- **wrap skirt 裹身裙** — 两片交叠系带，开衩处随步伐掀起一角 | *wrap skirt overlapping and tied, one panel lifting with each step*
- **tiered maxi skirt 层叠长裙** — 每层压一道荷叶边、逐层放宽，行走时层叠翻飞 | *tiered maxi skirt, ruffled rows widening downward, layers rippling as she walks*
- **denim mini skirt 牛仔短裙** — 硬挺合身，明线车缝与铜铆钉做旧 | *denim mini skirt, crisp fit, topstitching and aged rivets*
- **leather pencil skirt 皮铅笔裙** — 紧绷出光泽，折痕锋利 | *leather pencil skirt stretched sleek, sharp creases*
- **sequin skirt 亮片裙** — 亮片密排如鳞，光点随动作碎闪 | *sequin skirt scaled with glittering discs, sparks scattering with movement*
- **plaid schoolgirl skirt 格纹学院裙** — 褶裥对齐格线，裙摆随转身翻飞 | *plaid pleated skirt, checks aligned to every pleat, hem flying on turns*
- **culottes 裙裤** — 宽如裙、行走如裤，下摆垂至小腿，面料垂感 | *culottes wide as a skirt yet walking like pants, fluid hem at mid-calf*
- **bubble hem skirt 泡泡下摆裙** — 下摆收口鼓起，像收拢的花苞 | *bubble hem cinched and puffing, like a gathered bud*
- **ballerina wrap skirt 芭蕾裹裙** — 薄纱半裙在腰侧系结，透出腿线 | *sheer wrap skirt tied at the side, leg line veiled beneath*

## 连衣裙 · 日常
- **little black dress 小黑裙** — 贴合腰线、裙长及膝，剪裁极简但肩线领口精确 | *little black dress, nipped waist, precise shoulders and neckline, hem at the knee*
- **satin slip dress 缎面吊带裙** — 沿身体斜裁垂落，两条细肩带，胸口后背大片留白，液体光泽 | *bias-cut satin slip dress on thin straps, flowing liquid sheen, open lines at bust and back*
- **bodycon dress 包臀裙** — 从肩到膝无余量贴合，针织弹力勾勒曲线 | *bodycon dress mapped to the figure, stretch knit tracing every curve*
- **wrap dress 裹身裙** — 前身交叉系带，V领深至胸口，腰部收出曲线，裙摆散开 | *wrap dress crossing and tied, deep V neckline, curved waist, hem flaring*
- **shirt dress 衬衫裙** — 保留衬衫门襟与领，腰间束带收出比例，裙摆散至膝上 | *shirt dress with full placket and collar, belted waist, hem above the knee*
- **floral sundress 碎花太阳裙** — 轻薄贴身，裙摆到小腿，花朵印在轻透布面 | *floral sundress, weightless fabric to mid-calf, blooms printed on sheer cotton*
- **milkmaid dress 田园方领裙** — 方领贴住锁骨下缘，泡泡袖蓬起，缩褶胸口 | *milkmaid dress, square neckline below the collarbones, puffed sleeves, smocked bust*
- **corset midi dress 束腰中长裙** — 上身压出蜂腰，裙摆自胯部散开，缎面挺括 | *corset midi dress sculpting the torso, skirt flaring from the hips in structured satin*
- **off-shoulder dress 一字肩裙** — 落在手臂根部，锁骨肩线全露，袖口蓬起 | *off-shoulder dress below the arms, collarbones bare, sleeves puffing*
- **babydoll dress 娃娃裙** — 短而宽松，从胸线下散开，裙摆到腿根 | *babydoll dress loose from the bust, hem at the upper thigh*
- **turtleneck sweater dress 高领毛衣裙** — 贴合躯干到大腿，针织弹力显身形，罗纹收摆 | *turtleneck sweater dress tracing the body to mid-thigh, ribbed hem*
- **cutout dress 镂空裙** — 腰侧挖出对称缺口，皮肤与布料交错 | *cutout dress with symmetrical gaps at the waist, skin woven between fabric*
- **halter maxi dress 挂脖长裙** — 颈后系带后背全敞，裙摆拖至脚踝 | *halter maxi dress tied behind the neck, back fully open, hem to the ankles*
- **backless dress 露背裙** — 后背大开至腰仅交叉细带，转身露出整片背线 | *backless dress open to the waist with crossing straps, bare back revealed on turns*
- **sequin mini dress 亮片短裙** — 贴合身形，灯光下碎钻般闪烁 | *sequin mini dress fitted tight, glittering like crushed light*
- **bandage dress 绷带裙** — 条带交错缠绕躯干，收紧出雕塑感 | *bandage dress of interlocking strips, sculpted compression*
- **sweater dress 毛衣裙** — 粗针织垂坠到大腿中部，领口堆叠，下摆罗纹收 | *sweater dress in chunky knit to mid-thigh, pooling collar, ribbed hem*
- **leather mini dress 皮短裙** — 硬挺有型，折痕锋利泛哑光 | *leather mini dress structured and sleek, sharp creases in matte hide*
- **fairycore dress 仙气纱裙** — 多层薄纱从胸线飘散到小腿，微光点缀 | *fairycore dress, layers of gauze drifting from the bust, faint sparkle*
- **gothic lolita dress 哥特萝莉塔裙** — 黑色多层荷叶边与蕾丝层叠，束腰撑裙，缎带蝴蝶结点缀 | *gothic lolita dress in layered black ruffles and lace, corseted waist, ribbon bows*

## 礼服 & 婚纱
- **evening gown 晚礼服** — 自肩线一路垂坠至地，缎面在灯光下流动，裙摆拖地展开 | *evening gown falling from the shoulders to the floor, satin flowing under light, train spreading*
- **mermaid gown 鱼尾礼服** — 贴身至膝，膝下突然绽开成喇叭形裙摆，曲线毕露 | *mermaid gown hugging to the knee then flaring into a trumpet, every curve traced*
- **ball gown 大摆礼服** — 上身收束，腰下裙撑撑起巨大圆顶，层层纱裙如云 | *ball gown, corseted bodice, immense domed skirt in layered clouds of tulle*
- **high-slit gown 高开叉礼服** — 行走时腿部随开衩隐现，裙摆贴地拖行 | *high-slit gown, leg appearing and vanishing with each step, train trailing the floor*
- **tulle ball gown 网纱蓬裙** — 层叠如云雾，内层深外层浅，光下渐次透明 | *tulle ball gown layered like mist, deeper beneath, sheerer above*
- **wedding dress 婚纱** — 上身蕾丝密绣，裙摆从腰间铺开数米拖尾，头纱与裙摆同材质呼应 | *wedding dress, dense lace bodice, train spreading meters behind, veil echoing the same lace*
- **mermaid wedding dress 鱼尾婚纱** — 蕾丝贴合曲线至膝，膝下散开如花 | *mermaid wedding dress, lace mapped to the curves, flaring into bloom below the knee*
- **empire-waist gown 高腰礼服** — 腰线提到胸下，裙摆从高腰一路散开，希腊式垂坠 | *empire-waist gown gathered beneath the bust, Grecian drape spilling downward*
- **draped Grecian gown 希腊式礼服** — 单肩垂褶在肩头打结，布料随身体流动 | *Grecian gown knotted at one shoulder, fabric flowing with the body*
- **qipao-style wedding dress 旗袍式嫁衣** — 立领盘扣，金线刺绣爬满缎面，裙身贴身至膝下开衩 | *qipao bridal gown, mandarin collar and frog buttons, gold embroidery over silk, slit below the knee*
- **cocktail dress 鸡尾酒会裙** — 裙长过膝上身收束，裙摆微散，适配灯光社交场景 | *cocktail dress just past the knee, fitted bodice, hem softly flared*
- **beaded fringe flapper dress 20年代流苏裙** — 腰线低垂，珠链流苏随步伐摇曳碎闪 | *flapper dress with dropped waist, beaded fringe swaying and sparkling with each step*

## 文化 & 风格裙装
- **qipao 旗袍** — 立领盘扣，缎面贴合躯干曲线，侧开衩随步伐隐现腿线，滚边压出领线与襟线 | *qipao, mandarin collar and frog buttons, silk tracing the figure, side slit revealing the leg in motion, piping framing collar and placket*
- **modern qipao 改良旗袍** — 短袖蕾丝罩层，裙长缩短，剪裁松弛 | *modern qipao with lace overlay and shorter cut, relaxed tailoring*
- **hanfu ruqun 汉服襦裙** — 上襦交领、下裙高腰系带，裙幅宽大层叠，行走时裙摆如水 | *hanfu ruqun, crossover top and high-waisted tied skirt, wide layered panels flowing like water*
- **wide-sleeve hanfu 广袖汉服** — 广袖垂至膝下，抬臂时袖如流水滑落 | *wide-sleeve hanfu, sleeves to below the knee, pouring like water as arms rise*
- **mamianqun 马面裙** — 前后光面、两侧密褶，裙门织金纹样，行走时褶片开合 | *mamianqun, flat panels front and back with dense pleats at the sides, gilt motifs on the door panel, pleats breathing with each step*
- **kimono dress 和服式连衣裙** — 腰带在背后结出蝴蝶结，宽袖垂落 | *kimono dress with a bow-knot obi at the back, wide sleeves falling*
- **yukata 浴衣** — 棉布印花疏朗，腰带系结在身前，袖口宽大 | *yukata in airy printed cotton, obi tied in front, loose sleeves*
- **ao dai 奥黛** — 修身长衫两侧开衩到腰，内配宽腿长裤，行走时衣摆飘 | *ao dai, fitted tunic slit to the waist over wide trousers, panels floating mid-step*

## 裙装细节词
- **ruffle hem 荷叶边下摆** — 沿下摆层层翻卷，行走时如波浪起伏 | *ruffled hem curling in rows, waving with each step*
- **sunburst pleats 放射褶** — 自腰间向裙摆散开，褶幅越往下越宽 | *sunburst pleats radiating from the waist, widening toward the hem*
- **side slit 侧开衩** — 自膝上悄然划开，行走时隐时现 | *side slit opening quietly above the knee, glimpsed in motion*
- **handkerchief hem 手帕下摆** — 垂落多个尖角，随步伐摆动错落 | *handkerchief hem in falling points, staggering with movement*
- **sheer overlay 透视罩层** — 透视罩裙浮在衬裙外，边界朦胧 | *sheer overlay floating over the lining, edges dissolving*
- **train 拖尾** — 从裙后铺开长及数步，行走时铺展又收拢 | *train spreading steps behind, extending and gathering as she walks*
- **beaded fringe 珠链流苏** — 沿裙摆垂下，随动作摇曳碎闪 | *beaded fringe hanging from the hem, trembling with sparkle*
