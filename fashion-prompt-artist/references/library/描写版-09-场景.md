# 描写版 09 · 场景（具体描写）

> **场景描写公式**：空间主体 → 材质与色调 → 光线来源 → 氛围细节（天气/人流/声音感的视觉化）。
> 场景描写与人、光联动：先写空间，再让人物在其中占据构图位置。

## 影棚
- **white seamless studio 白棚** — 无缝背景连天地成一片纯白，影子被柔化成浅灰晕 | *white seamless studio, floor and wall fused into one white, shadows soft as grey haze*
- **grey gradient backdrop 灰渐变背景** — 中心亮、四周沉入深灰，人物浮在光晕中 | *grey gradient backdrop, bright core sinking into dark edges, subject floating in glow*
- **colored paper backdrop 彩色背景纸** — 高饱和纯色铺满，服装与背景成撞色 | *colored paper backdrop, saturated flat field clashing or matching the outfit*
- **black background studio 黑棚** — 只有轮廓光勾出人形，其余沉入黑 | *black studio, only rim light tracing the figure, everything else sinking into black*
- **studio with fan 风棚** — 风扇正对模特，发丝与衣摆全部向后飞 | *studio fan blasting the model, hair and fabric streaming back*

## 都市街头
- **city street crosswalk 城市斑马线** — 人流往来虚化，模特独立斑马线上，路面映着天色 | *busy crosswalk with blurred crowds, the model alone on the stripes, sky reflected on the asphalt*
- **Tokyo neon street 东京霓虹夜街** — 招牌层层垂落，光斑洒在湿地面上，夜里如彩色雨 | *Tokyo neon street, stacked signage, light pooling on wet asphalt like colored rain*
- **Hong Kong sign street 香港招牌街** — 密集招牌从楼体横出，光色交错市井烟火 | *Hong Kong street, signs jutting from the buildings, layered light and street life*
- **graffiti alley 涂鸦小巷** — 涂鸦铺满砖墙色彩饱和，模特倚墙成焦点 | *graffiti alley, saturated tags covering brick, model leaning in as the focal point*
- **rooftop 天台** — 俯瞰城市天际线晚霞铺满天幕，风掠过 | *rooftop over the skyline, sunset spreading across the sky, wind sweeping through*
- **parking garage 停车场** — 混凝土柱列规则排列，顶灯冷光，工业感 | *parking garage, columns in strict rhythm, cold overhead lights, industrial mood*
- **subway platform 地铁站台** — 站台灯白净隧道深处黑，列车进站带风 | *subway platform in white light, tunnel dark beyond, wind as the train arrives*
- **taxi backseat 出租车后座** — 皮革后座窗外城市光轨流动 | *taxi backseat, leather seats, city light streaks through the window*
- **convenience store 便利店** — 冷白灯光货架色彩密集，窗外夜街深蓝 | *convenience store in cold white light, dense colorful shelves, deep blue night outside*
- **laundromat 自助洗衣店** — 滚筒玻璃门内衣物翻滚，暖黄孤灯氛围 | *laundromat, clothes tumbling behind round glass, warm lonely glow*
- **café window seat 咖啡馆窗边** — 杯口蒸汽升起，窗外街道虚化流动 | *café window seat, steam rising from the cup, street blurred and moving outside*
- **library hall 图书馆** — 长桌台灯暖光书架高耸，尘埃在光柱中浮动 | *library hall, warm lamp pools on long tables, towering shelves, dust drifting in light*
- **art gallery 美术馆** — 白墙极简射灯洗墙，人影稀疏 | *art gallery, minimal white walls washed by spots, sparse visitors*
- **hotel lobby 酒店大堂** — 水晶灯垂落大理石地面反光，奢华空旷 | *hotel lobby, chandeliers spilling light, marble floor reflecting, opulent and empty*
- **night market 夜市** — 灯泡串横空，蒸汽与色彩弥漫人流熙攘 | *night market, bulb strings overhead, steam and color thick in the crowd*
- **diner booth 美式餐车卡座** — 红皮座椅窗外霓虹复古 | *diner booth, red vinyl seats, neon through the window, retro*
- **jazz bar 爵士酒吧** — 琥珀灯光吧台酒瓶反光烟雾朦胧 | *jazz bar in amber light, bottles glinting at the counter, haze soft*
- **backstage dressing room 后台化妆间** — 灯泡镜环亮，化妆品散落，幕后感 | *backstage vanity, bulbs ringing the mirror, cosmetics scattered*

## 自然 & 户外
- **sunset beach 日落海滩** — 落日熔金铺在海面浪线绵长，剪影感强 | *beach at sunset, molten gold on the sea, long wave lines, silhouette-ready*
- **salt lake 盐湖** — 湖面如镜天云倒影分不出虚实 | *salt lake as a mirror, sky and cloud doubled, horizon dissolved*
- **desert dunes 沙漠沙丘** — 曲线连绵风刻沙纹细密，光影分界锐利 | *dunes rolling in curves, wind-carved ripples, crisp light-shadow divide*
- **misty forest 雾中松林** — 树影渐次淡出，光束从枝隙垂落 | *misty forest, trunks fading in stages, beams dropping through branches*
- **bamboo forest 竹林** — 青翠密集风过沙响，光斑洒地 | *bamboo forest dense and green, leaf-shadow dappling the ground*
- **lavender field 薰衣草田** — 紫色延伸至地平线 | *lavender field running purple to the horizon*
- **wheat field 麦田** — 金色起伏，麦芒在逆光下镶金边 | *wheat field rippling gold, awns rimmed in backlight*
- **snow field 雪原** — 纯白无垠脚印是唯一线条，冷蓝调 | *snowfield endless white, footprints the only line, cold blue cast*
- **cherry blossom avenue 樱花道** — 花枝垂落头顶，花瓣飘落成粉雪 | *cherry blossom avenue, branches overhead, petals falling like pink snow*
- **alpine lake 高山湖** — 湖水蓝绿清澈雪峰倒影 | *alpine lake, clear blue-green water, snow peaks doubled in reflection*
- **greenhouse 玻璃花房** — 植物层层叠叠，阳光透过玻璃棱格洒下 | *greenhouse thick with plants, sun falling through the glass ribs*
- **cliffside coast 悬崖海岸** — 海风扑面裙摆发丝飞扬，脚下浪涛 | *cliffside coast, wind lifting skirt and hair, waves far below*
- **hot spring 温泉** — 蒸汽氤氲雪景或石景环绕，皮肤微红 | *hot spring in rising steam, stone or snow around, skin flushed*

## 建筑 & 旅行
- **cobblestone street 老城石板路** — 石板湿润反光建筑立面斑驳，街角转弯构图 | *old town cobblestones reflecting damp, weathered facades, turning corner composition*
- **Parisian Haussmann balcony 巴黎阳台** — 铁艺栏杆雕花，窗外屋顶铅灰 | *Haussmann balcony, wrought-iron railing, lead-grey rooftops beyond*
- **Venice canal 威尼斯水巷** — 碧绿河水船桨划开水纹，墙面色彩剥落 | *Venice canal, green water sliced by oars, paint peeling from walls*
- **Santorini 圣托里尼** — 白墙蓝顶层叠上坡地，光线炽白，几何纯粹 | *Santorini, white walls and blue domes stepped uphill, blazing light, pure geometry*
- **Moroccan riad 摩洛哥庭院** — 马赛克拼花拱门层叠，光从天井洒下 | *Moroccan riad, mosaic patterns, nested arches, light falling from the courtyard above*
- **torii gates path 千本鸟居** — 朱红连绵光从缝隙洒落，纵深感强 | *torii gates in vermilion rows, light squeezing through, strong perspective*
- **Jiangnan water town 江南水乡** — 白墙黛瓦乌篷船划过，石桥倒影 | *Jiangnan water town, whitewashed walls and black roofs, stone bridge doubled in the canal*
- **red wall 红墙** — 光斜照墙面树影投在朱红上 | *vermilion wall in raking light, tree shadow cast across*
- **cathedral interior 教堂** — 花窗把光染成宝石色，光斑铺在石柱地面 | *cathedral interior, stained glass tinting the light, colored pools on stone*
- **opera staircase 歌剧院楼梯** — 大楼梯弧线展开扶手镀金，灯影层层 | *opera grand staircase sweeping in curves, gilt railings, layered lamplight*
- **palace ballroom 宫殿宴会厅** — 水晶吊灯垂落镜面描金交错，空旷奢华 | *palace ballroom, chandeliers descending, mirrors and gilt interlocking, vast and opulent*
- **brutalist architecture 粗野主义建筑** — 混凝土几何切割，天光从缝隙切入，冷峻 | *brutalist concrete geometry, daylight slicing through gaps, severe and cool*
- **university cloister 学院回廊** — 拱廊纵列光斑成排 | *university cloister, arches in receding rows, light pooling in rhythm*

## 室内
- **bedroom morning 晨光卧室** — 光斜照进来床品起皱，尘埃在光柱中浮动 | *bedroom in morning light slanting in, rumpled bedding, dust floating through the beam*
- **walk-in closet 衣帽间** — 衣架色彩排列如色谱镜灯通明 | *walk-in closet, garments ranked like a color chart, mirror lights bright*
- **vanity table 梳妆台** — 镜灯环亮化妆品香水瓶排开 | *vanity table ringed with bulbs, cosmetics and perfume in rows*
- **fitting room 试衣间** — 窄小私密帘幕半开，镜中多重影像 | *fitting room, curtain half-open, layered mirror reflections*
- **flower shop 花店** — 花桶色彩密集水汽清凉，绿植垂落 | *flower shop, buckets dense with color, cool mist, greenery trailing*
- **ballet studio 芭蕾教室** — 镜面墙与把杆地板磨痕，光从高窗洒落 | *ballet studio, mirrored wall and barre, worn floor, light from high windows*

## 天气 & 时间
- **rain with umbrella 雨中撑伞** — 雨丝斜织伞面雨珠滚动，地面镜面反光 | *rain slanting down, umbrella beaded, pavement turned to mirror*
- **after rain 雨后** — 水洼映着天色空气清透 | *after the rain, puddles holding the sky, air rinsed clear*
- **snowfall 落雪** — 雪片缓落积在发肩，呼出白气 | *snow drifting down, settling on hair and shoulders, breath white*
- **windy day 大风天** — 裙摆发丝齐飞布料张力大 | *wind tearing through, skirt and hair flying, fabric pulled taut*
- **fog 浓雾** — 远景被吞没人物从雾中显形 | *fog swallowing the distance, figure emerging from the haze*
- **golden hour 黄金时刻** — 光线低斜万物镶金边影子拉长 | *golden hour, low raking light, everything rimmed in gold, shadows long*
- **aurora 极光** — 绿紫光带在夜空流动映在雪地 | *aurora ribbons of green and violet, reflected on snow*
- **milky way 银河** — 星河横贯天幕人物仰头 | *milky way spanning the sky, figure gazing up*
