# 描写版 11 · 镜头 · 色调 · 风格（描写公式与成品组合）

> 镜头/构图/风格类词条本身已是技术词，本文件给出**组合公式**与**成品组合段**，直接选用或替换变量即可。

## 🎯 镜头描写公式

```
焦段（决定透视）+ 光圈（决定景深）+ 对焦点 + 机位角度 + 画质质感
```

**成品组合（直接复制）**
- 人像铁三角：`shot on 85mm f/1.4, focus on the eyes, creamy bokeh, catchlights in the eyes` — 眼神有光、背景化开
- 全身时尚：`full-body shot, 50mm f/2.8, sharp focus head to shoes, centered composition` — 服装信息完整
- 环境人像：`35mm f/2, environmental portrait, subject in context, documentary feel` — 场景与人并重
- 压缩大片：`70-200mm at 200mm f/2.8, background compression, layers flattened` — 远景贴近人
- 动感街拍：`24mm f/4, low angle, dynamic perspective, slight motion blur on the hem` — 冲击力
- 细节特写：`macro 100mm f/4, detail shot of the fabric texture, razor-thin focus plane` — 材质质感
- 电影感：`anamorphic lens, oval bokeh, horizontal blue flares, cinematic still` — 宽银幕味
- 复古胶片：`Kodak Portra 400, fine film grain, faded warm tones, soft highlight roll-off` — 胶片味
- 直闪派对：`direct on-camera flash, hard shadows behind, night flash photography` — 冷感抓拍
- 高级棚拍：`medium format Hasselblad, f/8 deep focus, high-key studio, flawless rendering` — 商业级

## 🎨 色调描写公式

```
主色（大面积）+ 辅色（点缀）+ 光色（光源带来的色）+ 饱和度/对比倾向
```

## 🧠 万能组装示范（描写版全流程）

把「描写版」各文件的片段按顺序拼装：

```
[人物] beautiful young woman
[服装] ivory silk blouse, pearl sheen shifting with light + high-waisted wide-leg jeans falling straight from the hips
[配饰] gold hoops swinging from the lobes
[动作] caught mid-stride, legs crossing, hem swinging
[场景] old town cobblestones reflecting damp
[光线] golden hour, low raking light, shadows long
[镜头] 85mm f/1.4, focus on the eyes, creamy bokeh
[色调] Kodak Portra warm tones, gentle contrast
[风格] Parisian street style, candid motion
```

→ 成句：`RAW photo, beautiful young woman, ivory silk blouse with pearl sheen and high-waisted wide-leg jeans falling straight from the hips, gold hoops swinging from the lobes, caught mid-stride on damp old town cobblestones, golden hour raking light with long shadows, shot on 85mm f/1.4 with creamy bokeh, Kodak Portra warm tones, Parisian street style --ar 2:3 --style raw`

## 🔄 扩写任意名词的方法（从索引文件扩到描写）

以索引词「leather biker jacket」为例：
1. 廓形：合身/oversized？→「肩线利落，腰身短收」
2. 细节：拉链/铆钉/皮带扣？→「斜拉链错开门襟，银五金」
3. 材质光：哑光/高光/磨损？→「皮革磨出浅光泽」
4. 穿着状态：敞开/系上？→「敞开露出内搭」
5. 拼装：`biker jacket with offset zipper, silver hardware catching light, worn sheen, worn open over a slip dress`
