# 描写版 04 · 帽子 · 头饰（具体描写）

> **帽子描写公式**：形态（檐宽/顶形/包覆度）→ 材质光感 → 佩戴状态（压低/斜戴/后戴）→ 与脸、发、光影的互动（帽影/露发/遮眼）。

## 草帽 & 度假
- **wide-brim straw hat 宽檐草帽** — 檐缘比肩还宽，编织纹理细密，帽檐在脸上投下弧形阴影只露唇与下巴 | *wide-brim straw hat, brim wider than the shoulders, fine woven texture, arc of shadow across the face leaving lips and chin in light*
- **floppy sun hat 大檐软帽** — 檐缘柔软下垂，随头部动作轻颤 | *floppy sun hat, brim drooping softly, quivering as the head turns*
- **panama hat 巴拿马草帽** — 顶凹檐翘，编织细如缎 | *panama hat with a dipped crown and kicked brim, weave fine as ribbon*
- **boater hat 平顶硬草帽** — 平顶平檐轮廓硬挺，黑缎带绕檐 | *boater hat, flat crown and brim in crisp geometry, black ribbon band*
- **straw hat with black ribbon 黑缎带草帽** — 编织檐上黑缎带一线收束，度假感 | *straw hat banded in black ribbon, resort-ready silhouette*

## 毡帽 & 礼帽
- **felt fedora 毛毡礼帽** — 帽顶凹出纵向折痕，檐前压后翘，毡面哑光细腻 | *felt fedora, lengthwise pinch at the crown, brim down in front and up behind, matte felt*
- **wide-brim felt hat 宽檐毡帽** — 檐宽近肩，毡面吸光，帽影半掩眉眼 | *wide-brim felt hat nearly shoulder-wide, light-absorbing felt, brim shadow veiling the brows*
- **trilby 小檐爵士帽** — 檐短而翘，帽顶收窄，轻便复古 | *trilby with a short turned brim, tapered crown, light retro air*
- **cloche hat 钟形帽** — 贴合头骨包住耳朵，檐窄窄一圈，20年代轮廓 | *cloche hat hugging the skull over the ears, narrow brim, 1920s line*
- **pillbox hat 平顶小礼帽** — 立在头顶不遮发，轮廓几何 | *pillbox hat perched high, hair flowing beneath, geometric silhouette*
- **beret 贝雷帽** — 斜压在头顶一侧，羊毛呢面柔软服帖，帽底露出刘海 | *beret tilted on the crown, soft wool lying close, fringe escaping beneath*
- **french beret 法式斜戴贝雷** — 帽身堆出松软褶，一角坠向耳侧 | *french beret slouched into soft folds, one side drooping toward the ear*
- **top hat 高顶礼帽** — 高筒平檐，轮廓戏剧化 | *top hat, tall cylinder and flat brim, theatrical line*

## 休闲 & 运动
- **baseball cap 棒球帽** — 帽檐压低到眉上，布面挺括，马尾从后扣穿出 | *baseball cap brim low over the brows, structured front, ponytail through the strap*
- **wearing cap backwards 反戴棒球帽** — 檐口朝后露出全脸与刘海 | *cap worn backwards, face and fringe fully shown*
- **bucket hat 渔夫帽** — 檐缘窄软下翻，压低时只露唇角 | *bucket hat with a soft downward brim, lowered to leave only the mouth in light*
- **crochet bucket hat 钩织渔夫帽** — 镂空花纹透出发色 | *crochet bucket hat, openwork revealing the hair color*
- **beanie 毛线帽** — 包裹到耳上，罗纹翻折一道，头顶堆出软褶 | *beanie over the ears, ribbed cuff turned once, soft bunching at the crown*
- **pom-pom beanie 毛球帽** — 帽顶大毛球随步伐轻晃 | *pom-pom beanie, bobble bouncing with each step*
- **balaclava 针织头套** — 只露眼周轮廓，针织纹理贴合 | *balaclava framing only the eyes, ribbed knit mapped to the head*

## 复古 & 特色
- **newsboy cap 报童帽** — 帽顶分八片鼓起，前檐短小 | *newsboy cap puffed in eight panels, short front brim*
- **cowboy hat 牛仔帽** — 檐宽上卷帽顶凹陷，毡面哑光 | *cowboy hat, rolled wide brim and dented crown, matte felt*
- **fascinator 英式宴会头饰** — 立在头侧的网纱与羽毛，戏剧感 | *fascinator perched aside, netting and feathers, theatrical*
- **veiled hat with mesh face veil 面纱礼帽** — 网纱垂到鼻梁，透过纱面五官朦胧 | *veiled hat, netting to the nose bridge, features softened behind the mesh*
- **ushanka 苏联护耳毛帽** — 两侧耳扇放下，绒毛浓密包住脸颊 | *ushanka with ear flaps down, dense fur framing the cheeks*
- **turban 头巾帽** — 层层缠绕包裹，缎面反光结在额前 | *turban wound in layers, satin gleam, knot at the forehead*
- **silk headscarf 丝巾包头** — 缎面光泽包住发际系结在下巴下，复古出行感 | *silk headscarf wrapping the hairline, tied beneath the chin, glossy retro travel look*
- **1940s tilted hat 40年代斜戴小帽** — 小帽斜卡在卷发一侧 | *1940s hat pinned at a tilt over pinned curls*

## 发饰 & 冠饰
- **flower crown 花环** — 花朵与叶交错戴在头顶，花影落在发间 | *flower crown woven over the head, blooms and leaves casting petals of shadow into the hair*
- **pearl headband 珍珠发箍** — 珍珠串压住发际，光下点点珠光 | *pearl headband pressing the hairline, beads glinting softly*
- **wide padded headband 宽垫发箍** — 厚垫发箍撑起发顶，复古贵气 | *padded headband lifting the hair, vintage polish*
- **silk hair ribbon 丝带发饰** — 缎带系在发间垂下两条，随步伐摆动 | *silk ribbon tied in the hair, ends swaying with each step*
- **chinese hairpin 发簪** — 簪子斜穿过盘发，簪头垂着流苏步摇 | *hairpin slanted through the bun, tassel swinging from the tip*
- **hanfu hair accessories 汉服头饰** — 金步摇与珠花沿发髻排列，随步轻颤 | *hanfu ornaments along the bun, gold dangles trembling with each step*
- **tiara 头冠** — 细钻沿弧线贴合发顶，光下点点碎闪 | *tiara curving over the hair, diamonds sparking point by point*

## 🧩 组合示范
> 宽檐草帽：`wide-brim straw hat, brim shadow veiling the brows, lips in light` + `looking down with a soft smile` + `sunset beach, golden hour` → 神秘度假感。
