# 描写版 14 · 妆容（淡妆 · 浓妆 · 特殊 · 秀场 · 魅惑）

> **妆容描写公式**：底妆质地 → 眉 → 眼（线/影/睫）→ 颊（位置与色彩）→ 唇（质地与色）→ 整体光感。
> **性感原则**：雾面哑光=冷感高级，水光=娇媚；**眼与唇只强调一处**，另一处留白；魅惑感一半靠妆、一半靠「半阖眼神+微张唇」（见描写版-10）。
> **高级原则**：保留真实皮肤纹理（visible pores），色彩与服装呼应，位置克制。

## 🧴 底妆 & 皮肤质地（决定"高级"的第一层）
- **natural no-makeup makeup 裸妆** — 像没化妆的皮肤，只匀净提亮，毛孔隐约可见，光泽只从颧骨高点透出 | *no-makeup makeup, skin evened and brightened, pores faintly visible, glow only at the cheekbone highs*
- **dewy glass skin 水光肌** — 全脸水光，颧骨与鼻梁反光连成高光带 | *glass skin, light banding across cheekbones and nose bridge*
- **matte velvet skin 哑光绒肌** — 雾面无油光，粉感细腻，冷感高级 | *matte velvet skin, powdery fine, no shine, cool sophistication*
- **satin skin 缎光肌** — 介于雾面与水光之间，柔光如缎 | *satin skin, soft-focus sheen between matte and dewy*
- **editorial raw skin 原生肌** — 保留皮肤真实纹理与细小瑕疵，大片质感 | *editorial raw skin, real texture kept, nothing hidden*
- **wet-look skin 湿感肌** — 高光如淋水，眉骨颧骨亮片感 | *wet-look skin, highlights pooled like water on the brow bone*
- **sculpted contour 雕塑修容** — 侧影深、下颌线与鼻影锋利，脸如打光雕塑 | *sculpted contour, sharp jawline and nose shadow, face like lit sculpture*
- **bronzed sun-kissed 古铜日晒** — 暖棕从颧骨斜扫到太阳穴，像被阳光吻过 | *bronzed sun-kissed, warm bronze swept from cheekbone to temple*
- **porcelain fair 瓷白肌** — 均匀冷白，对比色妆容的画布 | *porcelain fair, even cool white, a canvas for bold color*
- **freckles kept 雀斑保留** — 底妆薄透，雀斑透出来，青春真实感 | *sheer base with freckles showing through*
- **blurred soft-focus skin 柔焦肌** — 磨皮般柔焦，纹理柔化不消失 | *soft-focus blurred skin, texture softened not erased*

## 🌷 淡妆（清透 · 高级 · 日常向）
- **clean natural makeup 清透淡妆** — 眉毛自然、睫毛只夹不浓、唇色接近本色，整体像"睡饱了" | *clean natural makeup, groomed brows, curled bare lashes, lips at their own color*
- **soft rosy makeup 蔷薇淡妆** — 眼影、腮红、唇用同一支蔷薇色扫过，气色浑然一体 | *soft rosy makeup, one rose tone washed over lids, cheeks and lips*
- **MLBB 伪素颜唇** — 唇色只比本色深半度 | *MLBB lips, half a shade deeper than natural*
- **inner-glow blush 水光腮红** — 腮红像从皮肤里透出来的红 | *blush glowing from beneath the skin*
- **sun-kissed blush 晒伤腮红** — 腮红横扫鼻梁连成一线，微醺阳光感 | *blush swept across the nose bridge, sun-kissed flush*
- **Korean gradient lips 韩式咬唇** — 唇心浓、唇缘雾化渐隐，像刚咬过樱桃 | *Korean gradient lips, color blooming at the center, edges misting out*
- **glossy nude lips 裸色水光唇** — 裸色打底加一层镜面唇釉 | *glossy nude lips, a mirror layer over bare tone*
- **soft brown liner 棕色内眼线** — 眼线只填睫毛根，棕色温柔 | *soft brown liner tight to the lash roots*
- **straight soft brows 自然平眉** — 眉毛顺其天然走向轻扫 | *brows brushed along their natural line*
- **one-product makeup 单品妆** — 全脸只用一支膏状腮红唇颊两用 | *one cream stick doing lips and cheeks, effortless*

## 🧩 高级妆容组合公式
```
[底妆质地] + [眉] + [眼·选1个重点] + [颊·位置] + [唇·选1个重点] + [光感呼应]
```
眼唇二选一做重点，另一处留白 —— 这是"高级感"的核心。

**6 个成品组合**
1. **老钱淡妆**：`natural makeup, luminous skin, soft brown liner, MLBB lips, glow at the cheekbone highs`
2. **好莱坞红唇**：`Hollywood glam, matte velvet skin, feline winged liner, classic matte red lip, sculpted contour`
3. **迷离烟熏**：`sultry smoky eye wrapped both lids, wet-look skin, glossy nude lips, half-lidded gaze`
4. **韩系水光**：`glass skin, straight soft brows, inner corner glow, Korean gradient lips, dewy highlight`
5. **创意大片**：`editorial raw skin, floating liner, gold foil tears below the eyes, blurred bare lip, gallery-like white line`
6. **秀场先锋**：`bleached brows, raw skin texture, closed-eye extension shadow, blurred lip, runway wet look`

## 🧩 与画面联动示范
> 魅惑晚宴人像：`sultry smoky eye wrapped both lids, glossy nude lips, wet-look skin` + `half-lidded gaze, lips parted`（描写版-10）+ `low-key lighting, rim light tracing the profile` → 高级魅惑张力。
