# 描写版 07 · 包袋 · 配饰（具体描写）

> **配饰描写公式**：形态与结构 → 材质光感 → 携带方式（拎/挎/夹/缠）→ 与身体线条/服装的互动。

## 包袋
- **quilted chain bag 菱格链条包** — 菱格饱满线缝利落，链条与皮肩带交叠肩头 | *quilted chain bag, plump diamond stitching, chain woven with leather on the shoulder*
- **clutch 手拿包** — 夹在指间，缎面或珠绣在光下细腻 | *clutch held between the fingers, satin or beading catching light*
- **beaded evening bag 珠绣晚宴包** — 珠面碎闪复古华丽 | *beaded evening bag glittering grain by grain*
- **tote bag 托特包** — 大而挺括皮面竖纹，内容物微露 | *structured tote, vertical-grain leather, contents just peeking*
- **canvas tote 帆布托特** — 布面磨软起褶，随性日常 | *canvas tote softened and creased, everyday ease*
- **straw basket bag 草编篮包** — 编织纹理疏朗提手缠绳，夏日感 | *straw basket bag, open weave and wrapped handle, summery*
- **baguette bag 法棍包** — 夹在腋下短肩带，包身修长 | *baguette bag tucked under the arm, slim silhouette on a short strap*
- **micro bag 迷你包** — 只容口红，链条细长斜挎，比例趣味 | *micro bag barely holding a lipstick, long fine chain, playful scale*
- **crossbody bag 斜挎包** — 带从肩划到胯，包身贴在髋侧 | *crossbody bag, strap slicing shoulder to hip, pouch at the side*
- **hobo bag 流浪包** — 软塌月牙形皮面垂坠起褶 | *hobo bag slumping into a crescent, leather draping in folds*
- **wicker bag 藤编包** — 纹理规律透着光 | *wicker bag, regular weave letting light through*
- **puffer bag 羽绒面包包** — 鼓软尼龙面哑光 | *puffer bag pillowy in matte nylon*
- **belt bag 腰包** — 扣在腰间实用利落 | *belt bag snapped at the waist, utilitarian line*
- **top-handle bag 手提包** — 提在指弯，硬挺结构感 | *top-handle bag carried in the crook of fingers, crisp structure*

## 腰带
- **corset belt 束腰宽腰带** — 压出蜂腰绑带交叉系紧 | *corset belt cinching the waist, laces crossed tight*
- **chain belt 链条腰带** — 绕在胯上环环相扣垂在髋侧 | *chain belt looped at the hips, links hanging over the bone*
- **obi belt 和风宽腰带** — 背后结出挺括结布面平整 | *obi belt knotting square at the back, fabric lying flat*
- **skinny belt 细腰带** — 扣在裤袢间一线收束 | *skinny belt threading the loops, one clean line*
- **western buckle belt 西部扣腰带** — 大金属扣头成为焦点 | *western belt, oversized buckle as the focal point*
- **sash belt 长飘带腰带** — 长带垂下随步伐摆动 | *sash belt, long ends trailing with each step*

## 丝巾 & 围巾
- **silk scarf 颈间丝巾** — 系在颈侧打小结，缎面折痕利落一角垂在胸前 | *silk scarf knotted at the neck, crisp satin creases, one end trailing*
- **scarf tied to bag 包柄丝巾** — 缠在包柄一角飘垂 | *scarf wound around the bag handle, one corner floating*
- **blanket scarf 毯式大围巾** — 绕颈两圈呢面厚实起绒，发丝压在围巾下 | *blanket scarf wrapped twice, wool thick and napped, hair tucked beneath*
- **pashmina 羊绒披肩** — 搭在肩臂垂坠到肘，绒面柔光 | *pashmina over the shoulders, draping to the elbows in soft glow*
- **fur stole 皮草披肩** — 搭在肩头毛峰蓬起 | *fur stole resting on the shoulders, pile standing full*
- **headscarf 头巾** — 包住发际在颌下系结，复古优雅 | *headscarf wrapping the hair, tied under the chin, retro grace*

## 手套
- **opera gloves 歌剧长手套** — 到肘上缎面贴臂泛柔光，手指修长 | *opera gloves to above the elbow, satin hugging the arms, fingers lengthened*
- **leather gloves 皮手套** — 贴合手背指节折痕细腻 | *leather gloves mapped to the hand, fine creases at the knuckles*
- **lace gloves 蕾丝手套** — 花纹透肤复古宫廷感 | *lace gloves, openwork over skin, courtly vintage*
- **fingerless gloves 露指手套** — 指尖露出便于动作，街头感 | *fingerless gloves leaving tips bare, street edge*

## 眼镜 & 腕表
- **cat-eye sunglasses 猫眼墨镜** — 上扬镜角拉出眼线感，镜面映出环境倒影 | *cat-eye sunglasses, upswept corners, environment reflected in the lenses*
- **oversized sunglasses 大框墨镜** — 遮住半张脸，深色镜面反光神秘 | *oversized sunglasses hiding half the face, dark mirror lenses*
- **aviators 飞行员墨镜** — 泪滴形镜片金属细框反光 | *aviators, teardrop lenses in thin metal frames*
- **90s slim oval sunglasses 窄椭圆墨镜** — 镜片细长贴眼，千禧感 | *slim oval 90s shades hugging the eyes, Y2K mood*
- **luxury watch 奢华腕表** — 表盘在腕间一点反光，抬手时金属闪 | *luxury watch, a glint on the wrist as the hand rises*
