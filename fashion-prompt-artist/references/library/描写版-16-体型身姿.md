# 描写版 16 · 体型 · 身姿（苗条 · 性感 · 多风格）

> **体型描写公式**：身高骨架 → 四肢线条（腿/臂/颈）→ 腰臀比例 → 肩颈背细节 → 动态仪态。五选三即可成段。
> **联动原则**：体型词与服装词互相成就——高个配拖地长裙、娇小配高腰短裙、沙漏配束腰、直角肩配挂脖、长腿配长靴/骑行裤。
> **控制体型**：正面加苗条系词；SD 负面提示词可加 `plus-size, overweight` 以稳定生成纤细体态。

## 📏 身高 & 骨架
- **tall model height 超模身高** — 175cm+ 的比例感，全身被拉长，任何服装都有余量 | *tall model height, everything elongated, clothes falling with room to spare*
- **tall willowy 高挑纤细** — 修长柔软如柳条，肩窄胯窄，线条一条到底 | *tall willowy frame, narrow shoulders and hips, one unbroken line*
- **svelte 高挑匀称** — 高而不单薄，比例匀停 | *svelte and balanced, tall without fragility*
- **medium balanced 中等匀称** — 165cm 左右的标准衣架，亲和感 | *medium balanced build, a natural hang for clothes*
- **petite 娇小** — 155-162cm，头身比紧凑，配高腰拉比例 | *petite frame, compact proportions, high-waisted styling stretching the line*
- **petite doll-like 娇小娃娃感** — 小骨架圆线条，可爱精巧 | *petite doll-like build, small bones, soft rounded lines*
- **leggy 长腿型** — 腿长过半身，腰线因此显高 | *leggy build, legs past half her height, waistline reading high*
- **short-waisted long legs 短腰长腿** — 上身短下身长，比例天生占优 | *short torso, long legs, proportions gifted*

## 🌿 苗条系（纤细 · 流畅 · 衣架感）
- **slender slim 苗条纤细** — 肩窄腰细，四肢线条流畅，无多余起伏 | *slender and slim, narrow shoulders, a fine waist, limbs in clean lines*
- **willowy 柳条身姿** — 修长柔软，动作带着摆动感 | *willowy, moving with a gentle sway*
- **svelte elegance 精瘦优雅** — 紧致不干瘦，衣物服帖 | *svelte, taut rather than thin, clothes sitting close*
- **lithe dancer's body 轻盈舞者** — 柔韧线条，背部与肩胛有控制力 | *lithe dancer's body, supple lines, controlled back and shoulders*
- **ballerina figure 芭蕾身型** — 长颈长臂长腿，绷脚背时线条延伸 | *ballerina figure, long neck arms and legs, line extending through pointed toes*
- **model-slim 模特瘦削** — 锁骨与肩胛清晰，天生的衣架 | *model-slim, collarbones and shoulder blades in relief, a natural hanger*
- **toned slim 紧致线条** — 有运动痕迹但不显壮，手臂腹部收紧 | *toned and slim, athletic hints without bulk*
- **slim hourglass 纤细沙漏** — 整体纤细但腰臀比清晰 | *slim hourglass, overall slender yet the waist-to-hip line clear*
- **streamlined 流线型** — 侧影薄，穿衣显瘦脱衣有线条 | *streamlined, thin in profile, definition up close*

## 🍑 曲线系（沙漏 · 性感张力 · 克制表达）
- **classic hourglass 经典沙漏** — 胸腰臀三围起伏明确，被束腰强调时最惊艳 | *classic hourglass, chest waist and hips in clear rhythm, stunning when cinched*
- **slim-thick 微丰沙漏** — 上身纤细、腰细、臀腿饱满，当代审美 | *slim-thick, slim up top, a fine waist, fuller hips and thighs*
- **curvy hourglass 丰润沙漏** — 曲线饱满但整体仍收束 | *curvy hourglass, generous curves held in a pulled-in frame*
- **nippled waist? no**
- **nipped waist 掐腰曲线** — 腰线被腰带/剪裁收至极细 | *a waist nipped in by belt and cut*
- **high waist + long legs 高腰长腿** — 腰线高、腿线长，S形自然成立 | *high waistline feeding into long legs, an effortless S-curve*
- **swan neck 天鹅颈** — 颈长而直，锁骨随之下移 | *a swan neck, long and straight, collarbones riding low*
- **elegant collarbones 锁骨线条** — 锁骨窝清晰，盛得住光 | *collarbones deep enough to hold light*
- **rounded shoulder line 圆润肩线** — 肩头线条柔和，挂脖款最佳 | *a soft rounded shoulder line, made for halter necks*
- **S-curve back 背部S线** — 背部曲线从颈到腰到臀连贯 | *an S-curve running neck to waist to hip*
- **back dimples 腰窝** — 腰后两点对称凹陷（露背装镜头）| *symmetrical dimples at the small of the back*
- **silhouette emphasis 轮廓强调** — 让服装剪裁替曲线说话：body-skimming / waist-defined | *let the cut speak: body-skimming, waist-defined*
- **leg line to ankle 腿线延伸** — 大腿到脚踝一条渐细的线 | *one tapering line from thigh to ankle*
- **delicate wrists & ankles 细腕细踝** — 末端骨感精致 | *delicate wrists and ankles, fine bones at the ends*

## 🎭 风格化体型（不同气质的苗条）
- **supermodel editorial 超模衣架** — 高挑瘦削，服装优先于人，冷感 | *supermodel frame, clothes leading, cool editorial distance*
- **k-pop idol figure 偶像匀称** — 直角肩、细腰、均匀线条，舞台感 | *idol figure, straight shoulders, a slim waist, even lines built for stage*
- **ballerina 芭蕾体态** — 挺背开肩，颈线永远延展 | *ballerina posture, back lifted, neck always extending*
- **dancer 现代舞者** — 柔韧与力量并存，动作里有弧线 | *modern dancer, flexible and strong, arcs in every move*
- **yoga toned 瑜伽线条** — 核心+背线，安静的力量感 | *yoga-toned, core and back lines, quiet strength*
- **vintage pin-up 复古沙漏** — 50年代三围比例，丰而不胖 | *vintage pin-up proportions, full curves on a small frame, 1950s*
- **office elegance 职场挺拔** — 站姿笔直，肩背打开，干练 | *office-elegant, standing tall, shoulders open, capable lines*
- **equestrian posture 马术体态** — 背直颈长，坐姿也带挺拔 | *equestrian bearing, straight back even seated*
- **old-Hollywood siren 好莱坞魅影** — 沙漏+天鹅颈+慢动作仪态 | *old-Hollywood siren, hourglass with a swan neck, unhurried poise*
- **idol petite 娇小偶像** — 娇小但比例精心打理，舞台上不显小 | *petite idol, compact but staged to read tall*

## 🦢 肩颈背细节（特写与半身镜头）
- **straight shoulders 直角肩** — 肩线平直，挂脖/一字肩的最佳画布 | *straight squared shoulders, the perfect canvas for halters*
- **long neck extension 长颈延展** — 下颌微收颈线上提 | *chin tucked, neck lengthening upward*
- **collarbone light 锁骨盛光** — 侧光时锁骨窝成两道小影 | *side light pooling in the collarbones*
- **shoulder blades 肩胛线条** — 背部微动时肩胛隐现（露背装）| *shoulder blades surfacing with movement*
- **arched back line 背弯** — 下腰时背线如弓 | *the back bowing when arched*
- **slim arms 纤细手臂** — 手臂细长，抬手时线条干净 | *slim arms, clean lines when raised*
- **defined waist shadow 腰侧阴影** — 侧光下腰窝凹出阴影 | *side light carving a shadow at the waist*

## 🦵 腿部细节
- **long legs 长腿** — 腿长占身高一半以上 | *legs over half her height*
- **slim thighs & calves 纤细大小腿** — 从大腿到小腿渐细 | *slim thighs easing into slim calves*
- **delicate ankles 细脚踝** — 踝骨清晰 | *ankles fine, bones defined*
- **pointed toes 绷脚背** — 芭蕾式脚背延伸腿线 | *toes pointed, the line extending through the foot*
- **high-heel line 高跟腿线** — 跟鞋让小腿肌肉收紧 | *heels tensing the calf into a cleaner line*
- **crossed-leg line 交腿线** — 坐姿交腿时腿线交叠出三角 | *crossed legs making a triangle of lines*
- **legs in motion 步态腿线** — 行走中前后腿交错成剪影 | *legs crossing in stride, a walking silhouette*

## 🧍 仪态 & 身姿语言
- **upright posture 挺拔站姿** — 头顶有线向上提，肩沉背直 | *a thread lifting from the crown, shoulders settled, back straight*
- **contrapposto 重心侧移** — 重心一条腿，髋侧送，身姿自然成S | *weight on one leg, hip eased aside, the body falling into an S*
- **relaxed elegance 松弛优雅** — 不用力的高级感，肩颈永远松 | *effortless elegance, shoulders and neck always soft*
- **confident stance 自信站位** — 双脚分开站稳，气场占满画面 | *feet planted wide, presence filling the frame*
- **graceful seated 坐姿优雅** — 坐如背有隐形线牵引 | *seated as if a thread pulls the spine*
- **runway bearing 秀场步态** — 直线行进，胯交替送出，肩稳头定 | *a runway bearing, hips leading in turn, shoulders steady*
- **slow, unhurried movement 慢动作仪态** — 一切动作慢半拍，贵气 | *every movement half a beat slower, unhurried grace*
- **leaning elegance 依倚身姿** — 倚墙靠柱时腰线依然挺 | *leaning yet never slumping, the waistline held*

5. **高挑御姐**：`tall svelte frame, swan neck, slim-thick curve at the hips, confident contrapposto, unhurried movement`

## 🧩 联动示范
> 长腿比例感全身照：`leggy build, legs past half her height, short torso` + `high-waisted wide-leg trousers`（描写版-03）+ `full-body shot, low angle`（描写版-11）→ 比例被镜头再次拉长。
> 沙漏晚礼服：`classic hourglass with a nipped waist` + `corset midi dress, skirt flaring from the hips`（描写版-02）+ `side slit` → 曲线被剪裁强调。
