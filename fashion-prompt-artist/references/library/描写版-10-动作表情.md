# 描写版 10 · 动作 · 表情（具体描写）

> **动作描写公式**：主姿态 → 次级细节（手/头/视线）→ 动态方向 → 衣物联动（裙摆/发丝/风的反应）。
> 动作与景别联动：特写选「回眸/托腮」，全身选「行走/旋转」。

## 站姿 & 行走
- **contrapposto 重心偏移站姿** — 重心压在一条腿，髋部侧送肩线反向微倾，身体呈S曲线 | *weight on one leg, hip shifted, shoulders tilting the other way, an S-curve through the body*
- **hand on hip 手叉腰** — 手肘外撑指尖扣在髋骨，腰线被强调出三角空隙 | *elbow out, fingers on the hip bone, a triangle of space at the waist*
- **over-shoulder glance 回眸** — 肩背对镜头脸转回望，锁骨颈线扭出张力 | *shoulder to camera, face turned back, collarbone and neck coiled with tension*
- **leaning against wall 靠墙** — 一侧肩背贴墙一腿屈膝点地，松弛中有支撑 | *shoulder against the wall, one knee bent to the ground, ease with structure*
- **arms crossed 双臂交叉** — 手指扣住对侧手臂，气场收敛有距离感 | *arms folded, fingers gripping the opposite sleeve, guarded presence*
- **walking towards camera 迎面走来** — 定格在双腿交错瞬间，裙摆与发丝向后飘 | *caught mid-stride, legs crossing, skirt and hair streaming back*
- **walking away 背影走远** — 背影前行裙摆荡开，可回头亦可不回头 | *walking away, hem swinging, head turned or not*
- **twirling 旋转** — 裙摆飞扬成圆，发丝随转体甩出弧线 | *twirling, skirt flaring into a circle, hair whipping an arc*
- **hair flip 甩发** — 发丝在空中划弧定格在飞起的刹那 | *hair flip frozen mid-arc, strands airborne*
- **coat billowing 大衣翻飞** — 下摆被风掀成三角，布料张力与身体线条相抗 | *coat hem torn into a triangle by wind, fabric straining against the line of the body*
- **catching petals 接落花** — 抬手接住落花指尖轻拈，视线专注 | *hand rising to catch a petal, fingertips just closing, focused gaze*
- **tiptoe reaching 踮脚够向** — 小腿绷直裙摆随抬升，向上张力 | *on tiptoe reaching up, calves taut, hem lifting*

## 坐姿
- **legs crossed sit 翘腿坐** — 脚尖勾出弧线上身微侧，手搭膝 | *legs crossed, toe drawing an arc, torso angled, hand resting on the knee*
- **knees together 并膝端坐** — 手交叠膝上端庄优雅 | *knees together, hands folded on the lap, composed*
- **side sit 侧坐** — 双腿收向一侧裙摆顺势铺开，手撑地面 | *sitting to one side, skirt spread along the floor, one hand propped*
- **windowsill sit 坐窗台** — 一腿屈一腿垂侧脸迎光 | *on the windowsill, one knee up one leg down, profile into the light*
- **hugging knees 抱膝坐** — 收拢成茧下巴抵膝，安静防备 | *knees hugged to the chest, chin resting down, quiet and closed*
- **elbow on knee 前倾撑膝** — 身体前探目光灼灼 | *leaning forward on the elbows, gaze locked*
- **lean back on hands 向后手撑** — 胸线展开迎着光 | *leaning back on the hands, chest open into the light*
- **stairs sit 坐台阶** — 双腿错层放下，视线向下或向镜头 | *seated on steps, legs staggered, gaze down or to camera*

## 卧 & 跪 & 大片
- **propped on side 侧卧撑头** — 腰线拉出长弧双腿交叠 | *lying on the side, elbow propping the head, a long waist curve, legs crossed*
- **arching back 下腰** — 背弯成弓发丝垂落铺开 | *back arching into a bow, hair spilling loose*
- **kneeling upright 跪立** — 上身挺直裙摆铺开环绕，颈线拉长 | *kneeling tall, skirt spread in a ring, neck lengthened*
- **crouching 蹲姿** — 踮脚手臂搭膝街头感 | *crouched low on toes, arms across the knees, street stance*
- **lying on back 仰卧** — 裙摆铺开成放射发丝散开 | *on the back, skirt fanned out, hair spread*
- **cobra pose 眼镜蛇式** — 上身撑起背线弯出张力 | *cobra pose, chest lifted, back under tension*
- **floating in water 水中漂浮** — 四肢舒展发丝在水中散开如墨 | *floating, limbs open, hair blooming underwater like ink*
- **runway walk 走秀** — 直线前行胯部交替送出，肩稳头定 | *runway stride, hips alternately leading, shoulders steady, chin level*

## 表情
- **over-shoulder gaze 回眸眼神** — 眼神带着钩子唇微启 | *glance over the shoulder, eyes hooked, lips parted*
- **fierce gaze 锐利眼神** — 直视镜头下颌微收 | *staring into the lens, jaw tucked, intensity*
- **soft smile 温柔浅笑** — 唇角轻扬不露齿，眼神柔 | *soft smile, lips closed and lifted, gentle eyes*
- **open laugh 开怀大笑** — 仰头露齿颈线与肩线舒展 | *laughing with the head back, teeth showing, neck and shoulders open*
- **eyes closed in breeze 闭眼迎风** — 发丝拂脸唇微张 | *eyes closed into the wind, strands across the face, lips parted*
- **side-eye 余光斜视** — 目光斜向镜头神秘挑逗 | *side-eye to camera, sly and magnetic*
- **dreamy gaze 梦幻远望** — 目光越过镜头落在远方，瞳孔虚焦 | *gaze drifting past the lens, focus soft and far*
- **biting lip 轻咬唇** — 眼神向下 | *lip caught between teeth, gaze lowered*
- **hat shadow face 帽影遮脸** — 只露唇与下颌，神秘 | *face in hat shadow, only lips and chin lit, mystery*
- **half in shadow 半面光影** — 明暗交界线划过鼻梁 | *half the face in shadow, the light line crossing the nose*
- **playful wink 俏皮眨眼** — 单眼闭合嘴角翘起 | *one eye winking, mouth corner lifted*

## 手部细节
- **fingers on collarbone 指尖锁骨** — 指节柔和弯曲 | *fingers resting soft on the collarbone*
- **hand in hair 手插发根** — 五指没入发间手肘抬起拉出线条 | *hand buried in the hair, elbow raised, line extended*
- **holding pendant 捏吊坠** — 拇指食指轻捏视线落在手上 | *thumb and finger on the pendant, gaze on the hand*
- **elegant hands 优雅手姿** — 手指自然舒展微曲如持无形羽扇 | *hands relaxed, fingers softly curved*
- **framing jaw 手托下颌** — 手背贴脸指并拢 | *back of the hand at the jaw, fingers together*
- **adjusting earring 调耳环** — 指尖捏住耳环头微侧露出颈线 | *fingertips on the earring, head tilted, neck bared*

## 🧩 组合示范
> 回眸+裙摆联动：`over-shoulder glance, shoulder to camera, skirt hem swinging` + `85mm f/1.4, focus on the eyes` → 张力人像。
