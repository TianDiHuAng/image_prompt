# 描写版 08 · 材质 · 面料（具体描写）

> **材质描写公式**：光泽类型（镜面高光/珠光柔光/哑光吸光/透光）→ 垂坠或挺度 → 表面细节（织纹/褶皱/绒毛/颗粒）→ 光影互动（反光面/吸光面/透光层）。
> 材质描写放提示词中段，与光线词条联动效果最佳（缎面配侧光、绒面配逆光）。

## 丝 & 缎
- **silk 真丝** — 贴身垂落，光泽随光线角度流转，褶皱处折出柔和高光带 | *silk falling close to the body, sheen shifting with the light, soft highlight bands in the folds*
- **satin 缎面** — 如液态金属，高光沿布面滑动，暗部沉稳 | *satin like liquid metal, highlight gliding across, shadows deep and calm*
- **duchess satin 重缎** — 挺括折痕锋利，光面近乎镜面 | *duchess satin structured, creases sharp, near-mirror sheen*
- **chiffon 雪纺** — 轻透起雾，层叠处透色，风过时飘起 | *chiffon airy and translucent, layers showing through, lifting in the breeze*
- **organza 欧根纱** — 挺立透明，折出玻璃质感的光边 | *organza stiff and clear, glassy light edges at every fold*
- **tulle 网纱** — 细密如雾，层叠处渐次透明，光下泛微光 | *tulle fine as haze, layers dissolving into transparency, faint glow*
- **velvet 天鹅绒** — 吸光深邃，转折处泛出丝绒光晕，抚过留下深浅痕 | *velvet drinking the light, halo glow only at the turns, brush marks shifting tone*
- **crushed velvet 压花丝绒** — 表面碎光斑驳，明暗交织 | *crushed velvet mottled with broken light*
- **brocade 织锦** — 纹样浮在缎面，金线在光下隐隐浮现 | *brocade motifs raised on satin, gold threads surfacing in light*
- **jacquard 提花** — 纹样立体，光下织纹起伏 | *jacquard with dimensional patterning, weave rising in the light*
- **taffeta 塔夫绸** — 挺括脆挺，褶皱锋利，纸感光泽 | *taffeta crisp, creases razor-edged, papery sheen*

## 蕾丝 & 刺绣
- **lace 蕾丝** — 花纹镂空透出底色，边缘扇形细密 | *lace with open florals over skin, scalloped fine edges*
- **Chantilly lace 尚蒂伊蕾丝** — 花枝蔓生，网底细如雾 | *Chantilly lace, trailing blooms on a mist-fine net*
- **eyelash lace 睫毛蕾丝** — 边缘纤须细密如羽 | *eyelash lace, feathery fine fringing*
- **guipure lace 线编蕾丝** — 无网底花纹相连如浮雕 | *guipure lace, motifs linked without net, relief-like*
- **eyelet 镂空绣花** — 孔洞规律排列，布面浮雕感 | *eyelet embroidery, perforations in tidy rows*
- **sequins 亮片** — 密排如鳞随动作翻转碎闪 | *sequins scaled tight, flipping into scattered sparks with movement*
- **beading 钉珠** — 颗颗立体光下珠光点点 | *beading raised bead by bead, glimmering points*
- **embroidery 刺绣** — 线迹起伏立体，丝线泛细光 | *embroidery in raised stitches, silk threads catching light*

## 针织 & 毛料
- **ribbed knit 罗纹** — 竖向沟壑光下纹理明暗交替，弹性贴合 | *ribbed knit, vertical ridges alternating light and shade, stretchy fit*
- **cable knit 麻花** — 绞花立体盘绕，粗线阴影深 | *cable knit, braids winding dimensional, deep shadows in the yarn*
- **cashmere 羊绒** — 细腻贴身绒面柔光触感如雾 | *cashmere fine against the skin, soft nap glow*
- **mohair 马海毛** — 绒毛罩面光下泛虹晕，轮廓虚化 | *mohair haloed in fuzz, faint iridescence, edges blurred*
- **bouclé 花呢圈圈纱** — 布面颗粒立体，光下细碎阴影 | *bouclé, nubby loops casting tiny shadows*
- **tweed 粗花呢** — 杂色点密布织纹粗粝，光下色点闪烁 | *tweed flecked densely, coarse weave, flecks glinting*
- **chunky knit 粗针** — 蓬松厚实线圈圈圈分明 | *chunky knit, loops distinct and plush*
- **pointelle 镂空针织** — 花纹细密透光如窗棂 | *pointelle, openwork fine as lattice*

## 棉麻 & 牛仔
- **poplin 府绸** — 细密平整哑光带微缎光，挺括 | *poplin smooth and even, matte with a faint sheen, crisp*
- **linen 亚麻** — 天然褶痕疏朗织纹粗细可见，纹理温润 | *linen with honest crumples, visible weave, warm texture*
- **cotton jersey 汗布** — 哑光贴身织纹细密，褶柔软 | *cotton jersey matte and close, soft folds*
- **denim 牛仔布** — 斜纹走向清晰，洗水处泛白，靛蓝深浅层叠 | *denim with clear twill lines, whiskered fades, indigo layers*
- **raw denim 原色牛仔** — 深靛近黑，折痕处磨出浅色 | *raw denim near-black indigo, wear blooming at the creases*
- **flannel 法兰绒** — 起绒表面格纹柔和触感温暖 | *flannel napped soft, plaid muted, warm to the eye*
- **corduroy 灯芯绒** — 条绒竖排光下条沟明暗交替 | *corduroy, wales in rows, ridges catching alternating light*
- **seersucker 泡泡纱** — 布面起泡纹理皱褶自然 | *seersucker puckered in natural waves*
- **chambray 钱布雷** — 轻软色经白纬织出淡蓝 | *chambray light and soft, pale blue from colored warp*

## 皮革 & 特殊
- **leather 皮革** — 表面细腻毛孔，折痕处泛柔光，皮质温润 | *leather with fine grain, folds glowing softly, warm hide*
- **patent leather 漆皮** — 高光如镜，反光锐利，暗部纯黑 | *patent leather mirror-bright, hard reflections, pure black shadows*
- **suede 麂皮** — 哑光绒面，色调随角度变深浅 | *suede matte and velvety, tone shifting with angle*
- **fur 毛皮** — 毛峰层叠光扫过毛尖泛银光 | *fur in layered pelts, silver sheen across the tips*
- **shearling 皮毛一体** — 皮面麂哑毛面卷曲蓬松 | *shearling, suede face and curly plush back*
- **latex 乳胶** — 高光如液态镜面，紧绷反光褶皱处高光锐利 | *latex in liquid-mirror gloss, stretched reflections, razor highlights at creases*
- **PVC 透明塑料** — 透出内层表面反光带塑料感 | *PVC transparent, inner layers showing, plastic sheen*
- **metallic foil 金属箔** — 如液态金属光下流动反光 | *metallic foil flowing like liquid metal*
- **holographic 全息** — 随角度变换虹彩紫绿粉流转 | *holographic surface cycling iridescence with angle*
- **glitter 闪粉** — 密布碎光光下星芒点点 | *glitter dusted dense, star points in light*
- **chrome 铬色** — 镜面映出环境倒影 | *chrome mirroring the surroundings*
- **mesh 网眼** — 细密透出底色光下如雾 | *mesh fine-veiled, base color showing through*

## 工艺形态
- **pleats 褶裥** — 排列规律光下褶片明暗成条纹 | *pleats in even ranks, light striping across the folds*
- **draping 垂坠褶** — 沿身体流动折痕柔软连续 | *draping flowing with the body, folds soft and continuous*
- **ruffles 荷叶边** — 层层翻卷边缘透光 | *ruffles curling in tiers, edges translucent*
- **smocking 缩褶绣** — 细褶密排成蜂窝纹理 | *smocking gathered into honeycomb texture*
- **quilting 绗缝** — 格块鼓起缝线凹陷利落 | *quilting puffing in blocks, seams recessed clean*
- **bias-cut 斜裁** — 布料沿曲线流动垂感如水 | *bias-cut fabric flowing on the curve, water-like drape*
- **twisted fabric 扭结面料** — 腰间拧出螺旋褶 | *fabric twisted into a spiral knot at the waist*
- **cut-out 镂空** — 缺口边缘利落，皮肤与布料交错 | *cut-outs with clean edges, skin interleaved with cloth*

## 🧩 组合示范
> 缎面+光线联动：`satin like liquid metal, highlight gliding across the folds` + `stripbox rim light, low-key studio` → 材质质感拉满。
