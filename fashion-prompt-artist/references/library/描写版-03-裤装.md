# 描写版 03 · 裤装 · 连体装（具体描写）

> **裤装描写公式**：腰线（高腰/低腰/花苞）→ 臀腿贴合度 → 裤筒宽度与裤脚处理 → 面料垂感/挺度 → 细节（口袋/车线/卷边/堆叠）。

## 牛仔裤
- **high-waisted jeans 高腰牛仔** — 腰头扣起提至腰最细处，臀部贴合、腿部宽松，比例被拉长 | *high-waisted jeans buttoned at the narrowest point, fitted hips, relaxed legs, proportions stretched*
- **wide-leg jeans 阔腿牛仔** — 从胯部直落，裤筒宽如裙摆，裤脚盖住鞋面只露鞋尖 | *wide-leg jeans falling straight from the hips, legs wide as a skirt, hems covering the shoes*
- **skinny jeans 紧身牛仔** — 从腰到脚踝无褶贴合，弹力布面泛哑光 | *skinny jeans mapped leg to ankle without a wrinkle, matte stretch denim*
- **straight-leg jeans 直筒牛仔** — 垂直落下裤线笔直，裤脚利落 | *straight-leg jeans dropping plumb, crisp vertical seams, clean hems*
- **flared jeans 喇叭牛仔** — 从膝下展开，裤脚扫过地面，70年代曲线 | *flared jeans kicking out below the knee, hems brushing the floor, 70s curve*
- **mom jeans 妈妈牛仔裤** — 腰高、臀部宽松、裤脚收窄，复古版型松弛 | *mom jeans high at the waist, roomy at the hips, tapered hems, vintage ease*
- **ripped jeans 破洞牛仔** — 膝盖横丝拉断露肤，磨白痕迹自然分布 | *ripped jeans, threads broken at the knees, natural whiskering*
- **cropped straight jeans 九分直筒** — 裤脚停在踝骨上方，露出一截脚踝 | *cropped straight jeans ending above the ankle bone, a strip of skin showing*

## 西裤 & 休闲裤
- **tailored trousers 剪裁西裤** — 裤线笔直烫出，垂坠到鞋面，腰头平整 | *tailored trousers with pressed creases, fluid fall to the shoe, flat waistband*
- **palazzo pants 阔腿长裤** — 垂坠如裙，行走时裤筒飘荡 | *palazzo pants draping like a skirt, legs billowing mid-stride*
- **paper-bag waist pants 花苞腰头裤** — 腰头系带收拢，腰间堆出松软褶 | *paper-bag waist pants cinched with a tie, soft bunching at the waist*
- **cigarette pants 烟管裤** — 贴合小腿，裤脚停在脚踝骨上方 | *cigarette pants slim to the calf, hems just above the ankle bone*
- **linen wide-leg pants 亚麻阔腿裤** — 布面带天然褶痕，透气松弛，垂中带挺 | *linen wide-leg pants with natural crumples, airy ease, drape with body*
- **cargo pants 工装裤** — 大腿两侧鼓着贴袋，抽绳收束裤脚 | *cargo pants with ballooning side pockets, drawcord hems*
- **joggers 束脚运动裤** — 大腿宽松、脚踝收紧，棉质起绒 | *joggers loose at the thigh, cuffed at the ankle, brushed cotton*
- **track pants 侧条纹运动裤** — 条纹沿裤线延伸拉长腿部，尼龙面微光 | *track pants with side stripes lengthening the leg, faint nylon sheen*
- **pleated front trousers 前褶西裤** — 前烫褶从腰头垂下，站立笔直、行走张开 | *pleated front trousers, creases hanging straight, opening with movement*
- **sailor pants 水手扣长裤** — 前襟双排水手扣排列，腰头阔挺 | *sailor pants with buttoned anchor flap, wide crisp waistband*

## 紧身 & 材质裤
- **leggings 打底裤** — 从腰到脚踝如第二层皮肤贴合，光面弹力 | *leggings fitted like a second skin, sleek stretch surface*
- **leather leggings 皮打底裤** — 紧绷出高光，膝弯折痕锋利 | *leather leggings stretched glossy, sharp creases at the knee bends*
- **leather straight pants 皮直筒裤** — 皮面随光线转折出深浅，裤线垂直 | *leather straight pants, sheen shifting with light, plumb legs*
- **velvet trousers 天鹅绒长裤** — 深色处吸光、转折处泛光 | *velvet trousers absorbing light, glowing only at the turns*
- **corduroy pants 灯芯绒裤** — 条绒竖排，光下纹理明暗交替 | *corduroy pants, wale lines vertical, alternating light and shade*
- **shiny disco pants 亮面迪斯科裤** — 高弹光泽紧贴，反射光随动作流动 | *disco pants stretched in high gloss, reflections sliding with movement*

## 短裤
- **denim shorts 牛仔短裤** — 裤脚毛边磨须，腰头利落 | *denim shorts with frayed hems, clean waistband*
- **tailored shorts 西装短裤** — 长至膝上，剪裁利落带烫线 | *tailored shorts to just above the knee, pressed creases*
- **bike shorts 骑行短裤** — 紧贴大腿弹力光面，与长靴/西装混搭 | *bike shorts hugging the thighs in sleek stretch*
- **micro shorts 超短裤** — 短至腿根，裤脚卷边 | *micro shorts at the upper thigh, rolled hems*

## 连体装
- **jumpsuit 连体裤** — 从肩直落腰间束带分段，阔腿裤筒垂坠 | *jumpsuit falling from the shoulders, belted waist breaking the line, wide fluid legs*
- **satin jumpsuit 缎面连体裤** — 液体光泽垂坠，挂脖领露背 | *satin jumpsuit in liquid drape, halter neckline, open back*
- **denim overalls 牛仔背带裤** — 背带搭在肩头，胸兜方正规整，裤腿宽松卷边 | *denim overalls, straps squared on the shoulders, neat bib pocket, cuffed wide legs*
- **romper 连体短裤** — 腰间收褶下摆散开，夏日轻快 | *romper gathered at the waist, flared shorts hem, summery ease*
- **boiler suit 工装连体服** — 口袋密布面料挺括，腰间收束 | *boiler suit pocketed everywhere, sturdy fabric, waist cinched*
- **catsuit 猫装连体衣** — 从颈到踝无褶贴合，曲线毕露 | *catsuit mapped neck to ankle without a wrinkle, every curve traced*

## 细节词
- **high-waisted 高腰** — 腰线提到躯干最细处，下装视觉拉长腿部比例 | *waistline at the narrowest point, legs visually lengthened*
- **floor-length pooling 裤脚堆叠** — 裤长盖过脚面，面料在脚踝堆出软褶 | *hems pooling over the shoes, soft folds stacked at the ankle*
- **raw hem 毛边裤脚** — 裤脚剪裁后磨出须边，松弛随意 | *raw frayed hems, undone ease*
- **contrast stitching 撞色车线** — 明线沿缝线延伸，与布面成对比 | *topstitching tracing the seams in contrast thread*
- **side stripe 侧条纹** — 条纹沿裤侧延伸，拉长腿部线条 | *stripe running the outer seam, leg line stretched*
- **drawstring waist 抽绳腰头** — 腰间抽绳系出蝴蝶结，腰头松软起褶 | *drawcord waist tied in a bow, waistband softly gathered*
- **suspender detail 背带细节** — 背带从裤腰越肩而过，胸兜点缀 | *braces crossing the shoulders from the waistband, bib pocket in front*
