# 描写版 06 · 首饰（具体描写）

> **首饰描写公式**：形态（链型/坠型/环型）→ 材质光感（金属反光/珠光/钻火彩）→ 佩戴位置 → 与身体光影互动（贴肤/晃动/折射）。

## 项链
- **pearl necklace 珍珠项链** — 单串珍珠贴在锁骨下方，珠面泛柔润珠光，颗颗圆润 | *single-strand pearl necklace resting below the collarbones, soft luster on every bead*
- **layered gold necklaces 叠戴金链** — 数条细金链长短错落叠在锁骨，链身细如发丝光下碎闪 | *layered gold necklaces at varied lengths, chains fine as threads, scattered glints*
- **velvet choker 天鹅绒颈链** — 紧贴颈根吸光沉稳，颈部线条被一线收束 | *velvet choker seated at the neck base, a single light-absorbing line*
- **lace choker 蕾丝颈链** — 花纹镂空贴颈，透出肤色，浪漫复古 | *lace choker, openwork against skin, romantic vintage air*
- **pendant necklace 吊坠项链** — 细链吊坠悬在胸口随呼吸轻晃 | *pendant necklace, charm suspended mid-chest, swaying with each breath*
- **Y-necklace Y形项链** — 从颈根垂下一条线，坠子在胸口正中收束视线 | *Y-necklace dropping in a single line, pendant steering the gaze to center*
- **statement necklace 主项链** — 铺满胸口宝石金属层叠，成为视觉焦点 | *statement necklace covering the chest, layered stones commanding the frame*
- **bib necklace 围脖式项链** — 覆满锁骨到胸口，宝石密排如铠甲 | *bib necklace armoring the décolletage in dense stones*
- **Cuban link chain 古巴链** — 粗链环相扣金属面反光强烈 | *Cuban link chain, heavy interlocked rings in strong reflection*
- **locket necklace 相盒项链** — 椭圆坠子带开合缝，复古怀旧 | *locket necklace, oval case with a seam, heirloom mood*
- **turquoise necklace 绿松石项链** — 珠串色如湖水，石面带天然纹理 | *turquoise necklace, lake-blue beads veined naturally*
- **rivière necklace 单排钻链** — 钻石沿颈线排开，颗颗碎闪 | *rivière necklace, diamonds lined along the throat, stone by stone sparkling*
- **charm necklace 吊饰项链** — 缀着小坠子随动作轻碰 | *charm necklace, tiny tokens clicking softly in motion*

## 耳饰
- **pearl studs 珍珠耳钉** — 贴在耳垂，柔光圆润低调优雅 | *pearl studs seated on the lobes, rounded soft glow*
- **diamond studs 钻石耳钉** — 细碎火彩在耳畔闪动 | *diamond studs flickering with fire at the ears*
- **gold hoops 金色圆环耳环** — 从耳垂垂下，金属面映着肤色 | *gold hoops swinging from the lobes, metal echoing skin tone*
- **oversized hoops 大圈耳环** — 垂至下颌线，晃动时拉长颈部线条 | *oversized hoops reaching the jawline, lengthening the neck in motion*
- **chandelier earrings 吊灯耳环** — 层叠坠到下颌，钻珠层层碎闪 | *chandelier earrings cascading to the jaw, tiers catching sparks*
- **tassel earrings 流苏耳环** — 丝线垂落随头转动轻摆 | *tassel earrings, threads swaying as the head turns*
- **ear cuffs 耳骨夹** — 扣在耳廓边缘，金属线条勾出耳形 | *ear cuffs clipped to the rim, metal tracing the ear*
- **drop earrings 垂坠耳环** — 单颗宝石悬在耳下，内里透亮 | *drop earrings, a single stone suspended, glowing from within*
- **mismatched earrings 不对称耳环** — 两侧形制呼应却各异，趣味点睛 | *mismatched earrings, paired yet unlike, playful accent*

## 戒指 & 手饰
- **stackable rings 叠戴戒指** — 数枚细戒叠在指节，宽窄错落 | *stackable rings crowded on one finger, widths alternating*
- **cocktail ring 鸡尾酒戒** — 大宝石占满指根，切面折射彩光 | *cocktail ring, an oversized stone throwing colored sparks*
- **signet ring 印章戒** — 戒面平整宽阔刻纹复古 | *signet ring, flat engraved face, heirloom feel*
- **midi rings 指节戒** — 戴在指骨中段细如线圈 | *midi rings at the knuckle, fine as wire loops*
- **bangles 手镯** — 多只叠在小臂行走时轻碰作响 | *bangles stacked on the forearm, clicking softly in motion*
- **gold cuff bracelet 金色开口镯** — 扣在腕骨金属弧面反光 | *gold cuff on the wrist bone, arc of metal reflecting*
- **tennis bracelet 满钻手链** — 贴腕排成一线光下连成星河 | *tennis bracelet lining the wrist, a river of sparks*
- **hand chain 手背链** — 从指环连到腕链，抬手时链线划过手背 | *hand chain linking ring to bracelet, a line drawn across the hand as it lifts*

## 身体饰 & 冠饰
- **body chain 身体链** — 沿锁骨到腰际斜挂，金属细链贴肤垂落 | *body chain slanting from shoulder to waist, fine links tracing skin*
- **belly chain 腰链** — 绕在腰际垂坠在髋部摆动 | *belly chain circling the waist, dangles swaying at the hip*
- **anklet 脚链** — 细链绕踝步履间轻晃 | *anklet circling the ankle, glinting with each step*
- **diamond tiara 钻石头冠** — 弧线贴合发顶，钻面碎闪 | *diamond tiara curving over the hair, sparks along the arc*
- **pearl brooch 珍珠胸针** — 别在驳领，一点柔光收束视线 | *pearl brooch pinned to the lapel, a soft point of focus*
- **ear-to-neck chain 耳颈链** — 从耳后划到颈侧，几何利落 | *ear-to-neck chain, a clean geometric line from ear to throat*
- **septum ring 鼻中隔环** — 细环垂在鼻中隔，金属微光 | *septum ring hanging fine, faint metallic glint*

## 材质光感词（叠加用）
- **baroque pearls 巴洛克珍珠** — 异形珠面流转虹晕 | *baroque pearls with irregular shapes, shifting iridescence*
- **rose gold 玫瑰金** — 暖粉金属光衬肤色 | *rose gold glowing warm against skin*
- **crystals 水晶** — 切面折射彩虹光斑 | *crystal facets splitting light into rainbows*
- **jade 翡翠** — 深绿温润内透光 | *jade deep green, glow held within*
- **enamel 珐琅** — 釉面色块平整如瓷 | *enamel set flat like porcelain*
- **gold with texture 金属肌理** — 拉丝/锤纹在光下细碎反射 | *brushed or hammered gold glinting in fragments*

## 🧩 组合示范
> 耳饰特写：`chandelier earrings cascading to the jaw, tiers catching sparks` + `extreme close-up, focus on the earring, dark background, rim light` → 珠宝广告感。
